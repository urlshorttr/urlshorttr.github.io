Two free tools can look almost the same on the surface: a box, a button and a short link at the end. The differences show up in the details. Which features cost nothing? Do you need an account? What do you get if you only want a quick link or a QR code? This page compares shorturl.at and urlshort.tr in plain terms, including the places where the other tool is stronger.

We run urlshort.tr, so keep that in mind as you read. We have tried to describe both tools fairly. Information about shorturl.at comes from its public homepage in October 2026. Plans change, so check their site for the latest details. We are not connected to shorturl.at in any way.

## The short version

Both tools shorten a long web address into a short one for free. The main differences are what comes with it. urlshort.tr gives you a custom ending, a QR code download and a click counter without an account. shorturl.at is built around an account and a paid plan, which adds a dashboard, detailed analytics, an API and more.

## Where urlshort.tr is the better fit

**You do not want an account.** There is nothing to register and no email to confirm. You paste a link and get a short one. This is ideal for a one-off link, a message to a friend, or a quick flyer.

**You want your own ending for free.** A readable ending such as `summer_menu` tells people what to expect before they click. On urlshort.tr you can choose one without paying. Our guide on [how to choose a custom short link ending](/blog/how-to-choose-a-custom-short-link-ending/) explains what makes a good one.

**You need a QR code.** Our [QR code generator](/qr-code-generator/) is free, has no watermark and draws the code in your browser. You can download it as a PNG for documents and screens or as an SVG for print. Before you print, read our [QR code size and contrast guide](/blog/qr-code-size-and-contrast-guide/).

**You want to see where a link goes.** Add a plus sign to the end of any urlshort.tr short link to open a preview page that shows the destination before you visit it. You can also use the [short link checker](/check-short-link/) for links from other services. This helps when someone sends you a short link you do not trust.

**You prefer your own language.** The tool pages are available in nine languages, so people can use them in the language they read best.

## Where shorturl.at is the better fit

**You need detailed reports.** shorturl.at lists a dashboard and detailed analytics as premium features. If you need to see which countries or devices your visitors come from, a tool with analytics is the right choice. urlshort.tr only counts clicks.

**You work with a team or need integrations.** shorturl.at lists an API, a UTM builder, a browser extension and app integrations. If you create links in bulk or from other software, those matter. urlshort.tr has no accounts and no API.

**You want an established service with support.** shorturl.at is operated by a domain company and lists support as part of its premium plan. urlshort.tr is a smaller, newer project.

## What our click counter does and does not do

Our [link click counter](/link-click-counter/) shows how many times a short link was opened. It stores only a number for each link. It does not save IP addresses and it does not build a profile of visitors. Search engine robots and link previews are not counted. Because some browsers and privacy tools block or prefetch links, treat the number as a close guide rather than an exact head count. Read more about this in our guide to [short links and privacy](/blog/short-links-and-privacy/).

## Which one should you choose?

- **Choose urlshort.tr** if you want a quick link, a readable ending, a QR code or a click total without making an account.
- **Choose shorturl.at** (or another service with accounts) if you need a dashboard, detailed analytics, an API or support.
- **Use either** for a simple link, but keep the original address somewhere safe, because a short link cannot be edited after it is made.

No short link tool is right for everyone. Our checklist on [what to look for in a free URL shortener](/blog/what-to-look-for-in-a-free-url-shortener/) will help you judge any of them, including ours.
