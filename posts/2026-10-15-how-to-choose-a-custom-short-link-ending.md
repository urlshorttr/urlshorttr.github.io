The last part of a short link, after the slash, is called the ending. A random ending such as `k3Zp9q` is fine for a one-off, but a custom one turns a short link into something people can recognise. Here is how to choose a good one.

## Why the ending matters

People judge a link in about a second. An ending like `team_survey` tells them what to expect. An ending like `k3Zp9q` tells them nothing and looks like the sort of thing a stranger might send. A readable ending raises the chance of a click, helps people remember the link and lowers the number of typing mistakes when the link is read from a screen or a printed page.

## The rules on this site

On urlshort.tr a custom ending can use letters, numbers and underscores, and must be between 5 and 30 characters long. Capital letters count as different from small ones, so `Menu_2026` and `menu_2026` are two separate links. A few words are reserved for the site itself and cannot be used. If the ending you want is already taken, you will see a message and can try another.

## Seven habits of good endings

**1. Describe the page.** `autumn_menu` beats `page1`. Think of the words a reader would use to describe where the link leads.

**2. Keep it short enough to type.** You have up to 30 characters, but the best endings are usually 8 to 16. A short link with a long ending defeats its own purpose.

**3. Use one separator.** Underscores are allowed, so use them between words, like `open_day_booking`. Do not mix styles within the same name.

**4. Prefer lower case.** Because capitals are treated as different, mixed case invites errors. If you print a link, a lower-case ending is easier to type from paper.

**5. Avoid look-alike characters.** The letter `l`, the capital `I` and the number `1` look alike in many fonts, and so do `0` and `O`. If a link will be printed or read aloud, choose words that avoid them.

**6. Add the year or version only if it helps.** `summer_sale_2026` is useful for a yearly event, but not if you plan to use the link again next year.

**7. Do not put private details in it.** A short link can be seen by anyone it is shared with. Avoid names, phone numbers, order numbers or anything personal in the ending.

## What to avoid

Do not copy a famous brand into an ending, for example `paypal_login`. It looks like phishing, can mislead people and may be removed. For the same reason, avoid endings that pretend to be official, such as `bank_verify`. Misleading names are against our terms and are exactly what makes people distrust short links.

## If your first choice is taken

Try these variations, in this order:

1. Add a word that says who it is for: `team_survey` becomes `staff_survey`.
2. Add a short qualifier: `menu_autumn`, `menu_lunch`.
3. Add the month or year: `menu_oct26`.
4. Reorder the words: `survey_team`.

Avoid adding random numbers, because they bring you straight back to an ending that is hard to remember.

## Examples

| Purpose | Weak ending | Better ending |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant menu | `link1` | `lunch_menu` |
| Event booking | `x8821` | `open_day_booking` |
| Customer survey | `survey` | `customer_survey_q4` |
| Product page | `new` | `trail_shoe_2026` |

## One thing to remember

A short link cannot be edited after it is made, so check the destination before you press the button. If the page moves, make a new short link and replace the old one wherever you used it. Then test the link yourself, and for printed material test the QR code with a phone as well.
