Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

The short version

You do not need an account. We do not keep a list of the links you shorten. The QR code is created in your browser. Advertising and the link service involve third parties, described below.

Links you shorten

When you press Shorten link, your browser sends the address you entered (and the custom ending, if you chose one) to is.gd, which creates the short link. That service has its own privacy policy. Do not enter addresses that contain private information.

QR codes

QR codes are generated on your device. The text you type into the QR code generator is not sent to us.

Data stored in your browser

To show your recent links, the site saves a short list of the links you made in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device. You can remove it with the Clear list button or by clearing your browser data.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party advertising at aboutads.info. See also how Google uses data from sites that use its services. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first.

Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested, under the GitHub privacy statement.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings. Some features of third-party services may then not work.
