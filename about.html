About URLShort

URLShort is a small, free website for two everyday jobs: making a long web address short, and turning a link into a QR code you can download.

What the site does

  • The URL shortener saves your address on our own link service and gives you a short urlshort.tr link for it.
  • The QR code generator draws the code in your browser and saves it as PNG or SVG.
  • The guides explain how to use both well.

What it does not do

There are no accounts and no click statistics. Links that are reported for scams, malware or other abuse are removed.

How it is paid for

The site is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads do not change what the tools do, and we do not take payment to review or recommend anything.
