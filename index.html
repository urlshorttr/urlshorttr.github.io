    When a short link helps

    Messages and profiles
    A short link takes less room in a bio, a text or a caption, and it does not wrap across three lines.
    Anything printed
    A QR code for a short link has fewer, larger squares than one for a long address, so it is easier to scan from a distance or on a small label. Read our print guide before you send a design to the printer.
    Slides, videos and podcasts
    People can type a short link from a screen, and you can say it out loud without reading a paragraph of characters.
    Tidying a messy address
    Links copied from shops, maps and search results often carry long strings after a question mark. A short link hides that clutter when you share it.

    What to know before you use it

    • Short links look like urlshort.tr/abc123 and send visitors to the address you entered.
    • A link cannot be edited after it is made. Check the address before you press the button.
    • Anyone who has a short link can open it. Do not shorten pages that are meant to be private.
    • You do not need an account. Links that are reported for scams, malware or other abuse are removed.
    • A short link hides the destination until it is opened. Our guide on checking short links explains how to look before you click.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this URL shortener free?

    Yes. There is nothing to pay and no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising.

    Do the short links expire?

    No expiry date is set. A link keeps working until it is removed, for example if it is reported for abuse. Keep the original address somewhere safe for anything important.

    Can I change where a short link goes?

    No. A short link points to the address it was made with. If the destination page moves, create a new short link and replace the old one.

    Do you track clicks?

    No. The service does not count clicks or build a profile of the people who open a link.

    Do you store the links I shorten?

    Yes, the address and its short ending are saved on our link service, because that is how the redirect works. There are no accounts. A short history of your recent links is also kept in your own browser, and you can clear it at any time.

    Will the QR code work on every phone?

    It is a standard QR code, so current phone cameras and scanner apps read it. Always test a code on a phone before you print it, and leave the white border around it in place.

    Can I use the QR code on printed material or products?

    Yes. Download the SVG if the printer asks for a vector file, or the PNG for documents and screens. Make sure you are allowed to link to the page the code points to.
