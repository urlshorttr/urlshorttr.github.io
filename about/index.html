About urlshort.tr

urlshort.tr is a small, free website that does two jobs well: it shortens web links, and it makes QR codes you can download as PNG or SVG. There are no accounts, no watermarks and no download limits.

Why this site exists

Most link tools ask you to sign up, put the result behind a dashboard or fill the page with clutter. We wanted a tool that opens fast, does the job in one step and explains plainly what it is doing with your data. The guides on this site are written to help people use short links and QR codes well, not just to describe our buttons.

What we do and do not do

  • A short link is stored on our own link service and redirects to the address you entered. We do not count clicks or build profiles of visitors.
  • QR codes are drawn in your browser. The text you type for the QR code generator is not sent to us.
  • We remove links that are reported as spam, phishing, malware or other abuse. Read the report page to flag one.
  • Links to other short-link services are not accepted, so a short link here never leads into a chain of redirects.

How the site is paid for

The site is free to use. It is supported by advertising, which is shown by a third-party advertising service. Ads never change what the tools do, and we do not take payment to place a link or a guide. Read our editorial policy for how the guides are written and updated.

Languages

The tool pages are available in several languages, which you can pick from the language menu at the top of every page. The guides and the legal pages are in English only, so that their meaning stays exact.

Contact

Questions, corrections and abuse reports are welcome. Use the contact page or write to contact@urlshort.tr.
