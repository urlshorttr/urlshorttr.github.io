title: Editorial policy description: How the guides on urlshort.tr are written, checked and updated, and how advertising is kept separate from them. ---

The guides on this site exist to help people use short links and QR codes safely and effectively. This page explains how they are made.

How guides are written

  • Each guide answers one practical question and is written for a general reader, in plain language.
  • Advice about QR codes is tested: we scan sample codes on current phones before recommending sizes or settings.
  • We do not copy text from other sites and we do not publish automatically generated pages.

Corrections and updates

When we find an error, or when something changes, we fix the guide and update its date. If you spot a mistake, please tell us.

Advertising

The site is supported by ads from a third-party advertising service. Ads are chosen by that service, are kept apart from the content, and do not influence what the guides say. We do not accept payment to recommend a product.

Translations

The tool pages are translated into several languages. The guides and policy pages stay in English so their meaning is exact.
