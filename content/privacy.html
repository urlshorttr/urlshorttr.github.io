title: Privacy policy description: What urlshort.tr stores, what stays in your browser, and how advertising and cookies work. ---

This page explains what happens to your information when you use urlshort.tr.

Links you shorten

When you press “Shorten link”, the web address you entered (and the custom ending, if you chose one) is sent to our link service and stored, because that is how the redirect works. There are no accounts. We count how many times each short link is opened and store only that number, which anyone with the short link can see on the click counter page. The counter does not save IP addresses and does not record who opens a link. Search engine robots and link previews are not counted.

To limit abuse, the link service temporarily uses your network address to count how many links one connection creates in an hour. We do not use it to identify you.

QR codes

QR codes are drawn in your browser. The text you type into the QR code generator is not sent to us.

Stored in your browser

The site keeps a short list of your recent links, and your language choice, in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device and you can clear them at any time (use “Clear list” on the home page, or clear site data in your browser).

Advertising and cookies

The site is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites, and to measure how ads perform. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your choice in a consent message before personalised ads are used.

  • You can manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics

The site may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies and shares visit data (such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type) with Google. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data.

Server logs

Our hosting and network providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) process technical data such as your network address and browser type when your browser asks for a page, for security and to deliver the site. We do not combine this with any other data.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Because we do not keep accounts or profiles, there is little personal data to access or delete. To ask about a stored link, or to have a link removed, write to contact@urlshort.tr.
