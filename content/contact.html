title: Contact us description: How to contact urlshort.tr about questions, corrections, removal requests or abuse reports. ---

We read every message. Replies usually arrive within a few working days.

  • General questions and corrections: contact@urlshort.tr
  • Harmful or abusive short link: use the report page. Please include the full short link.
  • Removal request for a link that points to your site: send the short link and a short explanation to the same address.

The form below opens your own email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent from this page until you press send in your email app.
