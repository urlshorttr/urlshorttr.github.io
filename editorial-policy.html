Editorial policy

The guides on this site are written to help you do one practical thing, such as check a short link or print a QR code that scans.

How we write

  • We explain things in plain language and give steps you can follow.
  • We base technical statements on the tools’ own documentation and on testing.
  • Rules of thumb are marked as rules of thumb.
  • No guide is written for payment, and ads do not influence the content.

Corrections

If you find an error, tell us on the contact page. We fix confirmed mistakes and update the article.

Updates

Guides are reviewed when the tools change, and the date on each article shows when it was published.
