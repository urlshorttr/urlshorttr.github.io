Report a short link

If a short link made on urlshort.tr leads to spam, phishing, malware, scams, illegal content or anything else that causes harm, tell us. We review reports and remove links that break our terms.

What to send

  • The full short link, for example urlshort.tr/abc123.
  • What is wrong with it, in a sentence or two.
  • Optional: how you found it. Please do not open a link you think is dangerous just to describe it.

Send the report to abuse@urlshort.tr, or use the contact page.

What happens next

  1. We look at the destination and at the report.
  2. If the link breaks the rules, we disable it so it no longer redirects.
  3. Repeated abuse from the same source is blocked.

Check a link before you open it

Add a plus sign (+) to the end of any urlshort.tr link to see where it goes first, or use the link checker. Our guide explains how to tell whether a short link is safe.
