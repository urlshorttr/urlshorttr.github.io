Check where a short link goes

Paste a short link from this site. You will see the full address first and can decide whether to continue.

Other ways to check

  • Add a plus sign (+) to the end of any short link from this site and open it. You get the same preview page.
  • This tool works for links made on this site only. For other services, look for their own preview option.
  • Read our guide on how to tell if a short link is safe.
